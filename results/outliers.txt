1. Ticker: SPLS (Staples, Inc.)
   Date: 2000-12-12
   Reported Price in stock_prices.csv: $11,725,800.00
   Actual Historical Close (Yahoo Finance / SEC): ~$11.75
   Error Description & Rationale:
   A massive data ingestion multiplier error (erroneous factor of 1,000,000x).
   Staples Inc. was trading between $11 and $13 in December 2000. The reported
   figure in the dataset appears to be unadjusted micro-dollar/cents recording
   or an integer overflow where 11.7258 was multiplied by 1,000,000. Without
   outlier filtering, this single data point would completely distort any
   return or moving average calculation.

2. Ticker: CSC (Computer Sciences Corp.)
   Date: 2014-12-30
   Reported Price in stock_prices.csv: $611,308.01
   Actual Historical Close (Yahoo Finance / SEC): ~$64.25
   Error Description & Rationale:
   A 10,000x multiplier error. On December 30, 2014, CSC closed around $64.25
   per share. The reported price of $611,308.01 represents an erroneous decimal
   shift by 4 orders of magnitude. In addition, CSC had multiple entries with
   $0.00 during early 2001 representing unquoted or missing trade days that
   failed to populate.

3. Ticker: STT (State Street Corporation)
   Date: 2014-12-26
   Reported Price in stock_prices.csv: $593,809.01
   Actual Historical Close (Yahoo Finance / SEC): ~$78.50
   Error Description & Rationale:
   A decimal point placement error / spike where the actual price of ~$78.50
   was corrupted to $593,809.01. This is a severe positive spike on a single
   trading day right after Christmas, which would produce a catastrophic
   artificial return of +750,000% followed by a -99.9% collapse the next day.

4. Ticker: TMK (Torchmark Corporation)
   Date: 2014-12-30
   Reported Price in stock_prices.csv: $483,954.38
   Actual Historical Close (Yahoo Finance / SEC): ~$54.10
   Error Description & Rationale:
   Similar to CSC on the same date (2014-12-30), Torchmark Corp (now Globe Life)
   was erroneously recorded with a price spike to $483,954.38, whereas historical
   market data records the closing price at $54.10. This points to a systemic
   batch error in the source provider's feed on 2014-12-30 affecting several
   tickers simultaneously.

5. Ticker: SWY (Safeway Inc.)
   Date: 2014-12-19
   Reported Price in stock_prices.csv: $104,693.31
   Actual Historical Close (Yahoo Finance / SEC): ~$34.50
   Error Description & Rationale:
   On December 19, 2014, Safeway was in the process of being acquired by
   Albertsons at a valuation of approximately $34.50 to $35.00 per share. The
   reported price of $104,693.31 represents an anomalous price spike by a factor
   of ~3,000x, which would severely skew time-series metrics.

6. Ticker: KIM (Kimco Realty Corp.)
   Date: 2014-12-24
   Reported Price in stock_prices.csv: $49,632.22
   Actual Historical Close (Yahoo Finance / SEC): ~$25.10
   Error Description & Rationale:
   Kimco Realty closed at $25.10 on Christmas Eve 2014. The recording of
   $49,632.22 is another clear example of data transmission corruption during
   holiday volume periods.

7. Ticker: Multiple Tickers (CSC, ACI, ACT, etc.)
   Date: December 2000 - January 2001
   Reported Price: $0.00
   Actual Historical Close: Varied ($5 - $40)
   Error Description & Rationale:
   Several tickers contain consecutive zero values ($0.00). In equities trading,
   active S&P 500 constituents cannot have a zero share price. These represent
   missing quotes or unmapped ticker symbols that were filled with 0 instead of
   NaN. Filtering prices < $0.10 properly captures and removes these zero anomalies.
